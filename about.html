Customer feedback & mystery shopping specialists.

Know Your Market is an expert provider of customer feedback and mystery shopping to companies across a range of sectors — helping you understand your business through the eyes of your customers.

Who we are

Insight you can act on.

We understand the significance of customer satisfaction and its direct impact on the growth of a business. Our goal is to help people just like you find out more about your own business through high-quality mystery shopping programmes, tailored to your exact needs.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality and attention to detail. Our extensively trained mystery shoppers possess a keen eye for observation and a thorough understanding of the specific criteria that determine exceptional customer service.

The result is actionable insight that delivers high return — and, in turn, grows our clients' businesses.
